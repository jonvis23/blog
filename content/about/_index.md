# **My Resume**

## Summary

I'm a **Network Security Engineer** who keeps distributed enterprise networks secure, observable and resilient. My path has run through IT infrastructure at **Absa Bank**, data analytics at **Safaricom** and now network security at **Calltek**, and the thread connecting them is the same: understand why a system behaves the way it does, then close the gaps before they become incidents.

Data is where I learned to read systems by their signals. Two years of chasing anomalies across large operational datasets taught me to look for root causes, not symptoms, and an access review I started on my own showed me how much risk sits in permissions nobody checks. That's what pulled me into security. Today I apply the same analytical discipline to network telemetry, configuration audits and incident response, and I'm growing toward cloud security and security engineering at scale.
